HTM — How To Make Tea

Three powders. Three different preparation styles.

01. Masala Chai Powder

Best prepared with milk for a rich, spicy cup.

01

Boil

Boil 100 ml water with 1 teaspoon masala chai powder.

02

Add Milk

Add 100 ml milk and simmer gently.

03

Serve

Strain into a cup and add sugar if desired.

02. Matcha Green Powder

Prepared by whisking the powder directly with warm water.

01

Scoop

Add 1 teaspoon matcha powder to a cup.

02

Whisk

Add 70–80 ml warm water and whisk until smooth.

03

Enjoy

Add more warm water or milk according to your taste.

03. Herbal Calm Powder

Prepared like an infusion for a lighter, gentler cup.

01

Heat

Heat 200 ml water until hot but not aggressively boiling.

02

Infuse

Add 1 teaspoon powder and steep for 4–5 minutes.

03

Strain

Strain and serve warm. Honey can be added if desired.
